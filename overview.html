Why we made it

Most people never notice user interface design. They rarely think about where a button sits or what color a line of text is. Yet these small choices decide whether a website can actually meet a person's needs. While EyeCon does not cover every WCAG 2.2 guideline, it introduces the core principles of alignment, contrast, and typography as a foundation for inclusive interfaces. And that's where good design starts.

About

So what is EyeCon?

EyeCon is an interactive 2D web-based simulation game for enhancing visual interface standards and accessibility literacy. You play as a junior UI designer who fixes broken interfaces for clients, from a personal portfolio to a mock-up of a Philippine government website.
